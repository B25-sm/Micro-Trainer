# What MicroTrainer Is For

## Our purpose

**MicroTrainer helps people learn technical skills the way a real trainer would — with practice, feedback, and someone watching their progress.**

We built it for **training batches** (colleges, bootcamps, coding programs) where:
- Students need to **practice daily**, not just watch videos
- Trainers need to **see who is active, who is struggling, and who is falling behind**
- Learning should feel **structured** (a clear path) but also **flexible** (practice interviews, coding, any topic)

---

## What we do (in plain language)

### For students

| What | Why it matters |
|------|----------------|
| **Guided courses** | Step-by-step lessons on topics like web dev, Python, databases — one concept at a time, with a quiz before moving on |
| **Browse any topic** | Not locked to one path — students can study any subject in the catalog |
| **Mock interviews** | Practice answering real technical questions, like a job interview |
| **Code practice** | Solve coding problems and run code in the browser |
| **Daily engagement** | Streaks, mini-assessments, badges — small habits that keep learning going |
| **Personal schedule** | A study plan: what to learn today, reminders when falling behind |
| **Progress dashboard** | Students see their own growth over time |

### For trainers

| What | Why it matters |
|------|----------------|
| **Student dashboard** | See the whole batch at a glance — who practiced today, who is at risk |
| **Individual student view** | Deep dive into one student’s progress, scores, weak areas |
| **Exports & reports** | Pull progress into spreadsheets for reviews or management |
| **Monitoring** | Know when students need a nudge before they drop off |

### For the organization

| What | Why it matters |
|------|----------------|
| **Structured learning paths** | Curriculum is defined per technology — consistent training across batches |
| **Accountability** | Activity is tracked; trainers are not guessing who did the work |
| **Scalable training** | One platform replaces scattered PDFs, random YouTube links, and manual check-ins |

---

## The problem we solve

**Before MicroTrainer:**
- Students watch content but don’t practice enough
- Trainers ask “who completed the assignment?” in WhatsApp groups
- No single place for lessons, interviews, coding, and progress
- Weak students are noticed too late

**With MicroTrainer:**
- One place to **learn → practice → get assessed → show progress**
- Trainers see **real activity**, not just attendance
- Students build **daily habits** and **interview readiness**

---

## Who uses it

| User | Role |
|------|------|
| **Student** | Learns, practices, takes quizzes and interviews, follows schedule |
| **Trainer** | Monitors batch, reviews progress, identifies at-risk students |
| **Admin / program lead** | Uses exports and overview for program health |

Students sign in with **Google or GitHub**. Trainers are a **fixed allowlist** of emails.

---

## What “success” looks like

- Students complete concepts and **unlock the next step** (guided mode) or **explore freely** (browse mode)
- **Streaks and assessments** keep people coming back
- Trainers open the dashboard and **immediately know** who needs help
- Mock interviews build **confidence before real placements**

## One sentence

**MicroTrainer is a practice-first learning platform where students build technical skills through guided lessons, coding, and mock interviews — while trainers see exactly how the batch is doing.**

## If you integrate us into another product

You are not integrating “a React app” — you are adding:

- **Structured technical training** for students
- **Trainer visibility** into batch progress
- **Daily practice habits** (engagement, schedules, notifications)

The other product handles its own users and UI; MicroTrainer handles **learning, assessment, and progress tracking** for coding/tech training programs.
